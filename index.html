My dearest Chanchal,
my Cherry 🍒

Happy Birthday, Madam Jii. 🎂💗 Aaj ka din mere liye sirf calendar ki ek date nahi hai. Aaj woh din hai jis din duniya ko tum mili thi, aur honestly, mujhe lagta hai ki duniya ko ek bahut hi pyaari, caring aur special person mili thi. Main kaafi der se soch raha tha ki tumhe birthday par kya doon, kya likhun aur kaise surprise karun. Phir mujhe laga ki kuch feelings ek normal “Happy Birthday” message mein kabhi fit hi nahi ho sakti. Isliye aaj main tumhe sirf wish nahi kar raha, main tumhare liye ek memory banana chahta hoon. 💌✨

Cherry, hamari story ki beginning bhi kitni random aur kitni beautiful hai. Sab kuch kisi movie ke perfectly written scene ki tarah start nahi hua tha. Na koi grand plan tha, na koi perfect introduction. Bas ek random WhatsApp number se baat shuru hui… aur dheere-dheere woh random beginning meri life ka ek important part ban gayi. Kab ek unknown person Chanchal bani, kab Chanchal meri Cherry bani, aur kab Cherry meri Madam Jii ban gayi, mujhe exact moment shayad kabhi pata hi nahi chala. Kuch connections dates se nahi, feelings se samajh aate hain. 🥹🍒

Aaj 3 September hai aur main peeche mudkar soch raha hoon ki hum approximately 25–26 ke around relationship mein aaye the. Exact date se bhi zyada important mere liye woh journey hai jo uske baad bani. Hum long distance mein hain. Hamare beech screens hain, kilometres hain aur Instagram chats hain. Lekin phir bhi tum meri daily life ka ek real part ban gayi ho. Ye strange bhi hai aur beautiful bhi—distance physically hota hai, lekin kuch log emotionally itne paas aa jaate hain ki distance unke connection ko define nahi kar pata. Tum mere liye waise hi ho. 💗

Mujhe tumhari bahut saari cheezein pasand hain, lekin tumhara care karna shayad sabse zyada special hai. Tum jis tarah care karti ho, woh kisi aur ko chhoti baat lag sakti hai, par mere liye nahi. Tum meri baat sunti ho, mujhe importance deti ho aur itni baar meri baat maan leti ho. Main perfect nahi hoon, aur kabhi-kabhi Sir Jii thoda annoying bhi ho sakta hai 😭, lekin tumne mujhe jis softness aur care ke saath apni life mein jagah di hai, woh mere liye genuinely precious hai.

Aur haan… mere kehne par photos bhej dena. 📸🥹 Shayad tumhe ye bilkul normal lagta ho, lekin long distance mein Madam Jii ki ek photo bhi Sir Jii ke liye sirf photo nahi hoti. Usmein tumhara ek moment hota hai—tumhari smile, tumhara mood, tumhari presence. Screen ke uss side tum hoti ho aur iss side main, aur beech mein woh chhota sa feeling hota hai ki distance ke bawajood tumhari presence mere paas hai. 💕

Agar mujhe hamari memories mein se ek moment choose karna ho jo unexpectedly mere dil ke bahut close hai, toh main tumhara woh voice note choose karunga. 🎙️🥹 Tumne bina maange achanak voice note bheja tha. Shayad tumhe uss waqt idea bhi nahi hoga ki mere liye woh kitna special ban jayega. Uski beauty hi ye thi ki woh planned nahi tha. Main expect nahi kar raha tha, maang nahi raha tha… aur tumne bas bhej diya. Kabhi-kabhi sabse beautiful memories wahi hoti hain jo humne plan hi nahi ki hoti. ✨

Hamari Instagram chats bhi mere liye sirf chats nahi hain. Random replies, notifications, serious conversations, normal nonsense, chhoti baatein… aur dheere-dheere wahi sab memories ban jaata hai. Technology kitni strange hai na? Ek app aur kuch messages kisi insaan ko itna important bana sakte hain. Lekin tumhari presence mere liye screen tak limited nahi hai. Tumhari baatein, tumhara care aur hamara connection meri everyday life ka part ban gaya hai. 💗

Chanchal… aur Cherry 🍒. Ye names mere liye sirf names nahi hain. Jab main Madam Jii sochta hoon toh automatically tum yaad aati ho. Aur jab tum Sir Jii bolti ho, toh ek stupid si smile aa jaati hai. 😭💕 Shayad kisi connection ki sabse beautiful cheez ye hoti hai ki do log apni chhoti si duniya bana lete hain—apne names, apne words, apni memories aur apne little moments ke saath.

Main jaanta hoon long distance hamesha easy nahi hota. Kabhi distance zyada feel hota hai. Kabhi bas mann karta hai ki screen ke through nahi, saamne baithkar baat ho. Lekin main unrealistic promises nahi karunga ki har din perfect hoga. Real connection ka matlab shayad ye nahi ki kabhi problems nahi aayengi; matlab ye hai ki problems ke baad bhi ek doosre ko samajhne ki intention rahe. Tum mere liye important ho, aur important logon ke liye effort diya jaata hai. 🤍

Aaj tumhara birthday hai. 🎂💗 Aur isi special occasion par main tumhe properly propose karna chahta hoon. Main chahta hoon ki aaj ka din sirf tumhare birthday ke liye yaad na rahe, balki hamari story ke ek beautiful moment ke liye bhi yaad rahe. Jab bhi future mein 7 September aaye, shayad tumhare mind mein ye thought aaye—“haan, isi din Sir Jii ne mujhe iss special tareeke se propose kiya tha.” 🥹💌

Tum already meri girlfriend ho aur main tumhara boyfriend hoon. Lekin kabhi-kabhi technically kisi relationship mein hona aur dil se ek special moment create karke apni feelings kehna, dono alag cheezein hoti hain. Maine tumhe kabhi iss tarah properly propose nahi kiya. Isliye mujhe laga ki tumhare birthday se better occasion kya ho sakta hai? Ek din jo pehle se special hai… usse hamari story se thoda aur deeply connect kar dena. 🎀

Main tumhe fairy tale future ka fake promise nahi dunga. Main ye nahi kahunga ki kabhi misunderstanding nahi hogi ya har moment easy hoga. Lekin main itna zaroor chahta hoon ki ham dono ke paas ek doosre ko lose karne ke bajaye samajhne ki intention rahe. Jab kuch galat ho toh ignore karne ke bajaye baat karne ki intention rahe. Aur life chahe jitni complicated ho, ek doosre ki importance yaad rakhne ki intention rahe. 💗

Tumhara care, tumhara meri baat sunna, mere kehne par photos bhejna, hamari chats aur woh unexpected voice note—ye mere liye random details nahi hain. Ye meri memories hain. Aur memories hi kisi connection ko real banati hain. Main future exactly predict nahi kar sakta, lekin aaj iss moment mein main tumhe choose karta hoon. Again. Intentionally. Happily. 💞

Toh meri Cherry 🍒, meri Chanchal, meri Madam Jii… aaj tumhare birthday par jab duniya tumhe celebrate kar rahi hai, main bhi tumhe celebrate karna chahta hoon. Tumhari existence ko. Tumhari smile ko. Tumhari care ko. Hamari random beginning ko. Hamari Instagram chats ko. Us unexpected voice note ko. Aur uss connection ko jo ek random WhatsApp number se shuru hua tha aur dheere-dheere meri life mein itni special jagah bana gaya. 🥹❤️

Maybe words kabhi enough nahi honge. Shayad ye letter bhi meri feelings ka sirf ek part hai. Lekin agar tum iss chitthi ko padhkar bas itna feel karo ki “haan, koi hai jo genuinely mujhe special maanta hai,” toh shayad meri baat tum tak pahunch gayi. 💌

So today, on your birthday, I am not asking a stranger to become mine. Main zero se kuch start nahi kar raha. Main bas un sab cheezon ko—hamari chats, care, names, memories, distance aur feelings—ek beautiful moment de raha hoon jo sirf hamara ho. ✨

And now, Madam Jii… there is only one thing left for Sir Jii to ask. 🥹💍

💍
ONE MORE QUESTION, MADAM JII

Chanchal,
my Cherry 🍒

You are already my girlfriend, and I am already your Sir Jii. But I never properly asked you in a moment like this.

So today, on your birthday—this special occasion that I hope stays connected to our story for a lifetime—I want to ask you something from my whole heart.

Will you continue choosing me, just like I choose you? 💗🥹
